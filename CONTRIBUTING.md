# Contributing

Open an [issue](https://github.com/GruheshKurra/awesome-ai-roadmaps/issues/new/choose) to suggest a pick or report a problem. Please use issues instead of pull requests for resources.

A new pick must:

- be free for its core content, with no login wall,
- be one specific video, paper, article or chapter (no playlists, channels, course homepages or other lists),
- teach the concept clearly, and be better than the current pick or fill a gap,
- come with one sentence on why it is better.

DSA and interview material is out of scope.

## Checks

```sh
python3 scripts/check.py
```

This validates the table format, duplicate links, and the link ledger. It does not judge teaching quality or check that remote links are live.
