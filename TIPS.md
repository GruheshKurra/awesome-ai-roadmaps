# Tips for learning AI faster

1. **Pick one resource per concept and finish it.** Switching between five explanations feels productive but delays understanding.
2. **Pause before the answer.** When a video poses a question or shows a derivation, stop and predict the next step. Then compare.
3. **Test yourself, then space it out.** Practice testing and distributed practice rated highest in a large review of learning techniques ([summary of Dunlosky et al. 2013](https://www.psychologicalscience.org/news/releases/which-study-strategies-make-the-grade.html)). Close the tab and explain the concept from memory the next day.
4. **Build the smallest version.** After "Backpropagation" or "Attention", write a 30-line version yourself. Compare with the lecture only when stuck.
5. **Read a paper in three passes.** Skim the title, abstract and figures first, then read the body, and only then work through the details ([Keshav, How to Read a Paper](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~brecht/courses/854-http-video-2012/readings/keshav-paper-reading.pdf)).
6. **Use shapes as a debugger.** Write the tensor shape next to every line when reading model code. Most confusion is a shape you did not track.
7. **Know when a video is enough.** For intuition, watch. For exact details, read the paper or code. For skills, build something.
8. **Keep a one-page notebook.** One line per concept: what it is, why it exists, one gotcha. Review it weekly.
